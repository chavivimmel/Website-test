Our Charitable Benevolent Purpose

Chavivim Volunteer Emergency Services Limited is a registered public benevolent institution established to provide free, immediate, and practical relief to individuals suffering from sudden distress, hazardous conditions, or physical helplessness.

Our trained volunteers respond swiftly to roadside breakdowns, vehicle lockouts, battery failures, severe weather hazards, and localized community crises—ensuring vulnerable community members are never left stranded or unsafe.

Our Crisis Relief Services

We provide rapid, zero-cost physical assistance to ensure safety and prevent harm during sudden roadside and environmental emergencies.

🚗

Roadside Hazard Protection

Providing safe positioning, traffic warnings, and technical aid for stranded motorists in dangerous highway or isolated locations.

  • Flat tire changes & pressure checks
  • Emergency jump-starts & battery checks
  • Fuel delivery to safe locations
🔐

Lockout & Entrapment Assistance

Immediate intervention for accidental vehicle lockouts, protecting vulnerable passengers, pets, and stranded drivers from dangerous weather exposure.

  • Non-destructive vehicle unlocking
  • Child & pet accidental lockout response
  • Key extraction assistance
🌧

Severe Weather & Storm Response

Deploying volunteer response teams during heavy storms, local flooding, and severe weather warnings to protect life and property.

  • Stranded driver extraction
  • Local storm debris clearing
  • Emergency transport assistance

How Our Dispatch Works

From the moment you call, our dedicated dispatch center coordinates rapid local volunteer response.

01

Call Our Dispatch

Dial (03) 4233 2200 to speak directly with our dispatch operators who will take details of your situation.

02

Location Assessment

Our dispatch team assesses your exact location, safety status, and immediate physical requirements.

03

Volunteer Dispatch

The nearest equipped volunteer response unit is deployed directly to your location to provide hands-on assistance.

04

Safe Resolution

Our team works on-site to resolve the issue safely and ensure you can resume your journey safely without cost.

Community Preparedness & Education

Empowering the public with knowledge and skills to prevent emergency situations and stay safe on the road.

📘

Roadside Safety Education

Educational outreach providing motorists with essential protocols on how to handle vehicle breakdowns safely while waiting for assistance.

📢

Extreme Weather Preparedness

Guidance and resources advising drivers on navigating severe weather alerts, flood risk management, and seasonal driving safety.
